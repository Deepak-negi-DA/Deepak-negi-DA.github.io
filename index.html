Why hire me?

Most consultants can write recommendations. I stay until they work — and then build the systems to prove it.

Ground-level diagnostic depth
I don't diagnose from slides. Every engagement starts with direct shopfloor observation — time studies, machine interviews, shift walk-throughs — before a single recommendation is made.
Data-to-decision bridge
I translate complex operational data into CXO-ready dashboards that management can act on. Power BI, Alteryx, AppSheet — the right tool for the right audience, every time.
Results that stick
I build SOPs, train teams, and embed systems so improvements outlast the engagement. The +17% throughput in steel wasn't a one-week win — it held.
Rare cross-sector breadth
Pharma GMP protocols, steel SMED, textile traceability, MDF PFMEA — I adapt quickly because the underlying problem-solving logic is the same across industries.
Digital-native mindset
I don't just recommend digitisation — I build it. AppSheet apps, Power BI architectures, and Google Apps Script automation deployed in live production environments.
Operator trust, executive fluency
I speak both languages. Operators trust me because I understand their machines. CXOs trust me because I translate outcomes into financial impact — clearly and without jargon.
